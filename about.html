About Me

Hi everyone!

Welcome to my website! Here you’ll find 2 distinct but related things. First, a big picture overview of myself, as well as the research I’ve engaged in, i.e. a resume, a list of publications, as well as this “About me” page. Second, you will also find the Toys n’ Sims section, a collection of some of the things I find beautiful. Math models which come from, or are tangentially related to, probability theory, all animated and interactive.

My whole life I’ve been drawing on graphs, and in an effort to save the trees, I’ve implemented an online alternative. The graph drawing tool can be used for fun (it has colors), but it can also be used to create custom domains to run the simulation on. Analyzing these models on regular, rectangular grids is hard enough, but simulating each one is (essentially!) equally difficult no matter which domain it’s being run on, so draw your own and see how it affects the behavior! As for the simulations themselves, each includes a description of what its purpose in life is, how to use it, and a list of sources if you want to start delving deeper.

Did you click on here to know about me? Hi, my name is Nadya, I am 23, a math and music nerd, currently living in New York City. For me, dialogue and stories have always been the most important parts of life, if through independent self-expression or community. However, life is a jumbled, interconnected mess, so telling more stories in any capacity naturally invites complexity into the conversation. In this vein, as I trudge along, some of the stories I’ve enjoyed have gotten progressively more abstract, in some cases lending themselves less well to pure written word and more to engaging directly with, say, large encompassing structures and rules. Other stories would engage with, say, the inseparable relationship between experience, emotions, and time, but that’s best left for a music section of this website.

For now I won’t spiel too much more about it and leave it here, all mathematicians are obsessed with structure, and the simple fact that structure can arise from randomness has fascinated me for as long as I can remember.

Welcome to my little garden, and enjoy your stay,
