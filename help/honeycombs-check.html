3D Tilings: Check Page

The sims can live on the 28 uniform tilings of 3-space: tilings by polyhedra with regular faces, meeting face to face, where every vertex looks like every other (B. Grünbaum, "Uniform tilings of 3-space", Geombinatorics 4 (1994), 49–56, List 1; #22, the cubes, is the cubic grid Z3). A sim's cells can be the tiling's vertices (neighbors when an edge joins them), its edges (neighbors when they share a vertex), its faces (when they share an edge) or its polyhedra (when they share a face).

Look at one

Colors: one for each type of cell (the polygons around a polyhedron's corner, or the polyhedra around a vertex, as the paper writes them).

    The checks

    For every tiling: each vertex has the polyhedra around it that List 1 says; the polyhedra come in the ratio that this says (the paper prints its own ratio too, shown next to it); every edge is 1 long and every face a flat regular polygon; the polyhedra of one block fill it exactly (their volumes add up to the block's), and so do the vertices' cells; each polyhedron has V − E + F = 2; every cell is its neighbors' neighbor, for all four kinds of cell; and every vertex sees the other vertices around it at the same distances. Then: the tilings the paper lists with the same vertex are really different, and boxes and tori of cells are built right. Press the button; it takes about a minute.
