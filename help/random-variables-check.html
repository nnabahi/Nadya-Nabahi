Random Variables: Check Page

These tests check the math behind the Random variables box: every law in the list against its formulas (its F against its density or masses, F−1 against F, its mean and variance), laws that are the same law made two ways, the special functions some laws need (the normal F, the Airy function, the Tracy–Widom laws, Minkowski's ?), the lines the box refuses, and the named random variables themselves: one number per name in each place, coupled sliders, independent names, random series and the many draws behind a formula's chart. Press the button; it takes about 10 to 20 seconds. The tests use fixed random seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
