Walker: Check Page

The sims with random walkers (Random Walk and the DLA family) share one walker. It lives on any lattice: the grids Z, Z2 and Z3, every tiling of the plane (its tiles or its corners as the cells, Penrose included) and every tiling of space. Each step goes to one of the cell's neighbors, all equally likely.

Far from anything that could stop it, the walker can instead jump: it skips all its steps inside a ball around it (the sites at most r steps away) and lands straight where it first leaves the ball. The chances of each landing place (the "exit law") are worked out once by solving a linear system, and by the strong Markov property the jump is exactly the same as taking the steps (details at the top of js/sim-walk.js). The checks below test that: exact answers on the line, the grids' symmetries, and the exit law against plain steps.
