Percolation: check page

These tests check percolation-clusters.js, the math behind both percolation sims: the clusters, wrapping around a torus, the sweep over every p, three exact facts about crossings and clusters on the square grid, and the chance of reaching the edge of a ball of a tree. Press the button; it takes about 10 to 20 seconds. The tests use fixed random seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
