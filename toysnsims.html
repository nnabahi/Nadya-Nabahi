Toys n' Sims

Interactive math simulations. Pick a genre to narrow the list.

Graph & Domain Tool

Paint a domain on a grid, wrap it into a torus, and save it for other sims to use.

Lattice Path Counting

Count the paths home to the corner through open edges, and watch the counts along a line turn into a bell curve, from above or in 3D.

  • Graph

Cellular Automata

Every cell looks at its neighborhood and changes by one rule, all at once: rule 30 on a tape, the Game of Life on a grid, or any rule number you type.

  • Automata
  • Coloring

Uniform Tiling

A uniformly random packing of rectangles, with or without gaps, in a box, a torus or a region you draw, mixed one small disk at a time.

  • Packing

Connected Coloring

A uniformly random coloring with N colors, each one connected piece, on a box, a torus or a region you draw.

  • Coloring

Sequential Tile Packing

Random points land in a domain one at a time, and each one grows a tile as big as it can until it touches the edge or an earlier tile, like a random Apollonian gasket, with any domain, tile and density you type.

  • Packing

Random Walk Coloring

N random walkers race across a box, a torus or a region you draw, and each cell takes the color of the first walker to reach it, in discrete or continuous time.

  • Coloring
  • Random walk

Sinusoid-ish

Put roots at random gaps and multiply: f(x) = ∏ (1 − x/R(n)) is a sine wave when every gap is 1, and bends as the gaps get random, with gaps from any set you type and histograms of the lobes.

  • Random walk

Eden Growth

Blocks drop one at a time on a uniformly random site of the base or just past it, and pile up into a mountain, on a line or a grid, with a tile you choose.

  • Growth

Sandpiles

The abelian sandpile: cells with too many grains topple onto their neighbors, in a box, a torus or a table you draw, with storms, avalanches and the table's identity.

  • Growth

Matrix Products

Multiply (1, 0) by randomly picked 2x2 matrices, over and over, and watch a cloud of runs grow, spin or settle into fractal dust, with any matrices, weights and formulas you type.

  • Random walk

Ising & Potts Model

Every cell has one of q colors and neighbors like to agree; slide the temperature through the critical point and watch blobs of every size form, with the heat bath or Swendsen–Wang.

  • Coloring

Generalized Voter Model

Cells change their opinion by looking at their neighbors, with any rule g you type: copy a random neighbor, follow the majority, go against it, or add noise.

  • Coloring

Bernoulli Site Percolation

Each cell is open with probability p; slide p and watch the open clusters join into one giant cluster at the critical point.

  • Graph

Bernoulli Bond Percolation

Each edge of the grid is open with probability p; on the square grid the giant cluster appears at exactly p = 1/2.
