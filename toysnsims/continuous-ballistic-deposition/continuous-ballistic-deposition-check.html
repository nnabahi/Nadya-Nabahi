Continuous Ballistic Deposition: Check Page

Each particle carries a tile straight down, its center at a uniformly random place across the floor, and stops the first time the tile touches the floor or a tile already there. The sim finds that place exactly, from the top of the difference set D = T − T above the line. The checks test D's top against a plain answer from the tile's own polygon, where each tile stops against a plain search over every tile, a big deposit recounted (no two tiles overlap, and each touches the floor or an earlier tile, found with the gap between tiles rather than with D), the surface, the uniform places, and coupling.
