Discrete Ballistic Deposition: Check Page

Each particle falls straight down a vertical line at a uniformly random place across the strip, and stops in the first cell it passes through that is next to the deposit or the floor. On the square grid that is the classic rule h(i) = max(h(i − 1), h(i) + 1, h(i + 1)) (on the cubic grid, the same with 4 neighbors), so the checks run the two side by side. On the other lattices, and in 3D, they test the pieces that make the rule exact: where a vertical line crosses the cells, where a particle stops against a plain search with no shortcuts, a big deposit and its surface recounted, and coupling.
