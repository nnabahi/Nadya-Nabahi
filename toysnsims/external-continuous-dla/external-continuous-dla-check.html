External Continuous DLA: Check Page

Each particle carries a tile in from infinity by Brownian motion and stops the moment its tile touches the cluster. The sim does this exactly, except that a particle stops within a tiny epsilon of touching and then slides into contact. The first check uses a case whose answer is known exactly: Brownian motion from infinity lands on a segment with the arcsine law. The others test each piece: the return to the launch circle against its Poisson kernel, the jumps across circles against Brownian motion in small steps, what epsilon changes, the gap between two tiles against a plain search, a big cluster recounted (every tile touches an earlier one, and none overlap), and coupling.

(about three minutes)
