External Discrete DLA: Check Page

Each particle comes from far away and walks, every neighbor equally likely, until it first reaches a cell next to the cluster, which then joins it. Where it lands has the harmonic measure from infinity of the cluster's edge. On the square grid that law can be worked out exactly from the walk's potential kernel, so the checks compare the sim with it: this tests the launch circle and the Brownian return, the sim's one approximation. On the other lattices, launches at two distances are compared with each other.

The rest is exact, and the checks test what makes it so: the Brownian return against its Poisson kernels, the jumps across balls against plain steps, the search for the nearest edge cell against a plain search, the cluster's shape and edge recounted, and coupling.
