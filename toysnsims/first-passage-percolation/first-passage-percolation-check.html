First Passage Percolation: Check Page

These tests check the math behind the sim: the passage times it finds from the Random variables box (js/sim-random.js), the random times it draws against their laws, Dijkstra's algorithm against the slower Bellman–Ford algorithm on every kind of domain, that the passage time is a distance, that exponential times grow the ball exactly like Eden's model (times on the cells) and Richardson's (times on the edges), that the times are coupled (the same seed, a growing box and a moving slider keep them in step), that a box that grows gives the whole plane's passage times, the holes of the ball, and that the pairs are uniform (first-passage-percolation-rule.js). Press the button; it takes about 10 to 30 seconds. The tests use fixed random seeds, so they do the same thing every time. The laws themselves are tested on the Random variables check page.
