First Passage Percolation: Check Page

These tests check the math behind the sim: the laws typed in the law box (js/sim-laws.js) against their formulas, the random times the sim draws against their laws, Dijkstra's algorithm against the slower Bellman–Ford algorithm on every kind of domain, that the passage time is a distance, that exponential times grow the ball exactly like Eden's model (times on the cells) and Richardson's (times on the edges), that the times are coupled (the same seed, a growing box and a moving slider keep them in step), that a box that grows gives the whole plane's passage times, the holes of the ball, and that the pairs are uniform (first-passage-percolation-rule.js). Press the button; it takes about 10 to 30 seconds. The tests use fixed random seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
