Internal Discrete DLA: Check Page

Each particle starts at the origin and walks, every neighbor equally likely, until it reaches a cell not in the cluster, which then joins it. For a few particles the law of the cluster can be worked out exactly: from a cluster A, the chance the walk leaves A at a cell y comes from the walk's Green's function in A (the average number of visits to each cell of A before leaving), which solves a small linear system. The checks compare the sim with those exact laws, on the line, the grids and other tilings.

In a bigger cluster the sim jumps across balls inside the cluster instead of taking every step. The checks test what makes that exact: the edge of the cluster (the empty cells next to it, the nearest of which says how big a ball is safe), and the clusters grown with jumps against clusters grown with plain steps.
