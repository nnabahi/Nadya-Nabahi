Ising and Potts: check page

These tests check the heat bath and Swendsen–Wang chains in ising-potts-chain.js, the math behind the sim: against exact probabilities on tiny graphs (where every coloring can be listed), and against Yang's and Onsager's exact answers for the Ising model. Press the button; it takes about 10 to 30 seconds. The tests use fixed random seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
