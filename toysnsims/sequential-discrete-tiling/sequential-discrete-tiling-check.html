Sequential Discrete Tiling: Check Page

These tests check the packing in sequential-discrete-tiling-packing.js, the math behind the sim: its quick search for the biggest squares around U must agree with trying every square; every tile must follow the rule; on small domains, every possible packing is listed with its exact chance, and many runs must match; and the Betti numbers it keeps must match a plain count. Press the button; it takes a few seconds. The tests use fixed seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
