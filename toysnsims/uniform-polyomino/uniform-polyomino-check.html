Uniform Polyomino: Check Page

These tests check the chain in js/sim-polyomino.js, the math behind the sim: every polyomino with 4, 5 or 6 cells is listed plainly, and the chain must visit each one equally often (or, with the sliders on, in proportion to its weight); without the touch rule it must not; and the numbers it keeps (perimeters, cycles, holes) must match a plain count. Press the button; it takes about 10 seconds. The tests use fixed seeds, so they do the same thing every time.
